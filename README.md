Rainbow Rush

Rainbow Rush is a fast, single-file browser action game about building an evolving unicorn herd, firing enormous rainbows, and surviving the Nightmare King's army.

Choose a difficulty, guide the herd between lanes, collect gates and power-ups, dodge hazards with magical backflips, and defeat the Nightmare King every ten waves.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern desktop or mobile browser. No installation or internet connection is required.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Move the mouse, Arrow keys, or A/D | Drag across the playfield |
| Jump dodge | Shift, Control, or Command (⌘) | Tap **JUMP** |
| Prism Blast | Space | Tap **BLAST** |
| Pause | P or the pause button | Tap the pause button |
| Mute | M or the speaker button | Tap the speaker button |
| Select mode | Keys 1–4 or click a mode | Tap a mode |
| Restart | Enter, Space, or click after a run | Tap after a run |

The herd automatically fires. Your horizontal position selects a lane, and auto-aim distributes incoming rainbows across available enemies in that lane.

## Modes

- **Dream** — approachable difficulty for learning the systems.
- **Nightmare** — faster and more dangerous enemy waves.
- **Apocalypse** — the most aggressive finite challenge.
- **Survival** — an endless run with escalating waves and a saved best-wave record.

## Herd and Evolutions

Growth gates add unicorns, increase rainbow power, improve firing speed, or multiply the herd. Evolution gates convert members of the herd into specialized unicorns:

- **Fire** adds rainbow damage as the Fire herd grows.
- **Storm** unlocks chain lightning between nearby Nightmares.
- **Celestial** creates wider rainbows with additional piercing.

Combining evolutions unlocks powerful build synergies. The lead Alpha Unicorn also levels up as you pass gates, increasing base rainbow power and gaining a more impressive appearance.

## Active Abilities

- **Jump Dodge** launches the entire herd into a high rainbow-trailing backflip. The herd is temporarily invulnerable, can clear Storm hazards, and earns streak rewards for jumping over projectiles. Its cooldown prevents repeated use.
- **Prism Blast** fires a devastating wide rainbow and clears hostile projectiles. It has a longer cooldown.

Successful attacks and dodges build a score streak. Higher streak tiers multiply points and reduce ability cooldowns.

## Enemies and Bosses

Enemy formations, shields, fast attackers, crossfire, minibosses, and road hazards demand movement between lanes. Minibosses are tougher but move more slowly than ordinary enemies.

The Nightmare King appears every ten waves and uses several distinct attacks:

- Fast aimed shots
- Splitting projectiles
- Crossfire patterns
- Predetermined curved and wavy shots
- Rare homing shots that track briefly and then lock, allowing a last-second fake-out
- More aggressive attacks after becoming enraged

## Power-ups and Challenges

Runs can include shields, Mega Rainbows, freezing, bombs, all-lane homing, mirrors, rescues, Golden Unicorns, mystery rewards, no-hit challenges, kill contracts, and Storm survival events.

**Bullet Time** combines rapid firing with deliberate slow motion: enemies and hostile projectiles slow down while player rainbows continue at full speed.
